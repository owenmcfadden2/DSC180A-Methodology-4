## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorm

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic we’ve covered in our domain this quarter (so far) is dealing with multicollinearity within our data. Our Quarter 1 Project deals with archetyping college basketball players based on a combination of features describing their stylistic preferences. A huge issue we ran into off the rip was dealing with features that are correlated purely by the nature of the data. For example, if we’re using shooting by zone of the court as a feature, shots from zones near each other will be correlated naturally (i.e. a player shooting from two adjacent zones will likely be correlated). To deal with this, we used the idea of “centroids” to map nearby zones to coordinates further apart.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

We already think we have our Quarter 2 Project lined up in conjunction with our Quarter 1 Project, which is to use our Quarter 1 player archetypes as well as newly formed team archetypes (Quarter 2) to project transfer performance. The idea here is that teams and coaches should find players who will mesh well with their system rather than solely relying on perceived talent and past production.
